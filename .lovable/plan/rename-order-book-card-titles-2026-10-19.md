# Rename Order Book card titles

On the Order Book Report screen and its PDF only (Open Sales Orders stays unchanged):

- LD Status → **LD**
- Open Orders by Sales Zone → **Orders By Zone**
- Plant-wise Pending → **Plant-wise order received**
- Top 10 Open Orders by Customer → **Top 10 Orders by Customer**
- Top 10 Open Orders by Product → **Top 10 Orders by Product**
- Open Orders by Model Wise → **Top 10 Orders by Model Wise**

## Technical details
- Switch these titles on the existing Order Book variant flag in the shared dashboard, for both the screen and the PDF layouts.
